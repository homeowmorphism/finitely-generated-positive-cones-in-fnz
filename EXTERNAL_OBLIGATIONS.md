# External proofs: completed

All required external obligations E00–E02 and O00–O01 are proved locally. A citation is not used as a Lean theorem. The exact theorem and axiom checks are in `ProofAudit.lean` and `DynamicsAudit.lean`.

- **Exact base cone:** `External/BaseCone.lean` proves exclusion of identity and totality for the supplied strict semigroup generated by `{aGamma, bGamma}` in `⟨a,b | ba²b=a⟩`. The replacement uses an explicit piecewise linear action, positive central fractions, and checked word normalization. The identity-adjoined monoid is confined to the totality proof.
- **Noncofinal center:** `NoncofinalEndpointAction.center_cofinal_of_isolated` proves cofinality for every isolated product left order on a nonabelian countable free basis. It uses a central-orbit endpoint and opposite finite-trace motions of a common conjugate. This bypasses the changing-primitive trajectory-bound issue discussed in the historical notes below; that issue is no longer a required obligation.
- **Isolated circular orders:** `FnZPositiveCones.circularOddRankObstruction` in `CircularIsolatedComplete.lean` proves the exact odd-rank obstruction. Actual successors, invariant last-letter accumulation sets, cyclic component stabilizers and tight orbit gaps supply the density needed for closed proper-suffix avoidance and the protected crossing argument. The finite normalized-domain endpoint calculation then forces even rank.
- **Full product theorem:** `External/OddRankComplete.lean` proves `mmrt_no_isolated_order_odd : LeftOrderOddRankObstructionGoal` and `oddRankObstruction : OddRankObstructionGoal`. Neither assumes finite positive generation, cofinality, circular separation, or another external theorem.

The source correspondence is Navas's marked base-cone theorem; MMRT Proposition 4.12, Theorems 1.1 and 1.3; Mann–Rivas Proposition 4.7 and the cofinal quotient construction; and the closed-suffix repair corresponding to Matsumoto Lemma 7.2. `blueprint.md` describes the mathematical replacements. Independent reviews are in `ENDPOINT_ACTION_REVIEW.md`, `INVARIANT_COMPONENT_REVIEW.md`, `CIRCULAR_SEPARATION_REVIEW.md`, and `STRONG_OBSTRUCTION_REVIEW.md`.

All audited proofs depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. No additional computational trust mechanism is used. The direct FG classification remains independently available.

## Historical development notes

The following entries preserve the implementation history. Statements that a branch was open describe earlier checkpoints and are superseded by the completed status above.

E00–E02 are proved in `PositiveConesBlueprint/External/BaseCone.lean`.

- `base_one_not_mem : BaseOneNotMemGoal` uses an explicit piecewise linear action on
  the ordered real line. Its marked maps satisfy `b * a^2 * b = a`, `a 0 = 1/3`,
  `b 0 = 0`, and `b (1/3) = 1/6`. Every nonempty positive product moves zero right,
  or fixes zero and moves one third left. Thus it cannot be the identity.
- `base_total : BaseTotalGoal` first writes every group element as a positive word
  times an integer power of `a^3`. A checked finite-word normalizer removes `aaa`
  and `baab` blocks. The resulting word and its complement in one central positive
  step lie in the temporary monoid `{1} ∪ basePositiveClosure`. Splitting the central
  exponent gives the required strict semigroup totality statement.
- `base_positive_cone : BaseConeGoal` combines these results. The temporary monoid
  is confined to the totality proof; the final cone is the supplied Subsemigroup.

This replaces the projective ping-pong proof of [Navas, Proposition 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0909.4994)
with the explicit action, and implements the normal-form argument of §§1–2.
No faithfulness assumption or external citation is used as a theorem.

## The cofinal isolation transfer is proved

`External/Cofinal.lean` constructs unique integer floors and fundamental
representatives. `External/CircularQuotient.lean` uses them to construct an actual
left-invariant `CircularOrder` on the first factor of a product with `ℤ`.
`External/CircularCocycle.lean` proves the normalized 0/1 cocycle identity for
any left-invariant circular order, including every repeated-point case.

`External/CocycleExtension.lean` constructs the central extension, its strict
positive semigroup, and an explicit product splitting over a free group, with
both inverse laws verified. `External/CocycleTopology.lean` proves continuity
and injectivity of this lift when generator heights are fixed.
`External/CircularInjectivity.lean` recovers the full circular order from its
cocycle; `External/CircularTopology.lean` uses the actual topology of ternary
Boolean signs to prove the circular-order lift continuous and injective.

`External/CofinalCocycle.lean` identifies the actual carry cocycle with that of
the quotient circular order. With generator heights equal to the original
integer floors, the lifted cone is exactly the original cone. Consequently
`isOpen_singleton_cofinalProductCircularGroupOrder` proves the required
isolation transfer. No finiteness assumption on the free basis is needed.
`External/CofinalTopology.lean` separately proves openness of the positive
cofinal locus in finitely generated groups. `Cone/Opposite.lean` verifies the
sign-reversal homeomorphism needed to orient the cyclic generator positively.

This supplies a substantive replacement for the compressed topological argument
in [MMRT §4.2, p. 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.02348). Continuity and local
injectivity of the quotient map alone transfer isolation in the opposite
direction, as in [Mann–Rivas, Proposition 5.4, p. 1442](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.3191.pdf).
The implementation proves the needed direction by a continuous injective lift
through the given order and the preimage of its open singleton. It no longer
leaves a local-section or openness assumption unproved.

## Checked pieces of the remaining dynamical branches

The finite parity endpoint is checked as `MMRTParity.even_of_one_boundary` in
`External/OddRankObstruction.lean`. It takes actual finite permutation data:
a rotation with `n+1` cycles, a free involution pairing `4*K` darts, and a single
full boundary cycle. Permutation signs prove `Even n`. Constructing those data
from an isolated circular order remains unproved.

The infinite-free-rank subcase is proved in `External/InfiniteRank.lean`:
`InfiniteFreeRank.not_finitelyDetermined` applies to `FreeGroup ι × H` for every
infinite generator type and every group `H`. An involution inverts an unused
generator while fixing all prescribed finite signs. This implements MMRT
Lemma 4.13, but does not prove the finite-rank noncofinal case.

`External/ConvexExtension.lean` extends arbitrary cone changes inside a convex
subgroup while retaining all outside signs, without normality. Finite
determination descends to that subgroup. `External/FiniteCentral.lean` strengthens
this to finite semigroup generation, constructs the convex subgroup bounded by
central powers, and proves that its group and free projection are finitely
generated when the ambient positive cone is. None of these results implies
that the bounded subgroup equals the ambient group.

`ODD_ROUTE_REVIEW.md` records primary-source checks of alternative approaches.
Regular positive languages occur in every rank, and finite positive generation
does not make central intervals finite. Infinite index does not imply infinite
free rank for a nonnormal subgroup. These shortcuts cannot discharge the two
remaining inputs.

The pinned mathlib supplies circular orders, permutation signs, circle lifts,
and a ping-pong injectivity criterion. Source inspection found no checked

theorem. Citations and Python certificates are not used as proofs.

obstruction hypothesis. `CircularComponentConfiguration` derives actual
component permutations and their endpoint actions. `CircularNormalizedOrbit`
places each colored orbit closure inside its open normalized domain, so all

complement of that orbit closure, using the unique successor-gap label. Every
